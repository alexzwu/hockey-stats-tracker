# 🏒 Hockey Stats Spotter

A fast, mobile-friendly ice hockey game stats tracking web application designed specifically for youth hockey parents, team managers, and coaches.

---

## 🌐 Live Web App (Access on iPhone)

You can open the app directly on your iPhone Safari from anywhere:
👉 **[https://alexzwu.github.io/hockey-stats-tracker/](https://alexzwu.github.io/hockey-stats-tracker/)**

To install it on your iPhone:
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. The app is now installed on your iPhone like a native app and **works 100% offline** even at ice rinks with no cellular or Wi-Fi signal!

## 🎯 Key Features Built for Fast-Paced Games

### 1. Visual "Spotter" System (Solving the Player Identification Problem)
* **Never squint at names again:** During fast play, helmets, ponytails, and tucked jerseys hide player names. This app puts **huge, high-contrast jersey numbers** front and center.
* **Grouped by Lines & Pairings:** Filter by **Line 1 (F)**, **Line 2 (F)**, **Line 3 (F)**, **D-Pair 1**, **D-Pair 2**, or **Goalies**. When a line hops over the boards, your brain only has to track 3–5 numbers.
* **Visual Cue Tags:** Tag players with visual cues like *"Pink stick tape"*, *"White laces"*, *"LH stick"*, or *"Tinted visor"* to instantly spot them early in the season.

### 2. Fast 2-Tap Tracking & Instant Undo
* **Tap Player Number $\rightarrow$ Tap Stat Button.** Logged in under 2 seconds.
* **One-Tap Undo:** Prominent **Undo** button in the header that displays the exact player and event being undone in case of a misclick during a chaotic scrum.
* **Opponent Quick Tally:** Quick buttons to log opponent shots and goals without slowing down your team's tracking.

### 3. Fully Customizable Events
Go to the **Settings** tab to toggle stats on or off:
* Track only what your coach cares about (e.g. Shots on Goal, Goals, Assists, Saves).
* Turn off high-speed stats like Completed Passes or Faceoffs until you feel comfortable.
* Create **Custom Events** (e.g., *"Zone Exit"*, *"Dump-in Recovered"*, *"Puck Battle Won"*) with your own custom short codes.

### 4. Post-Game Exports
Under the **Stats & Export** tab:
* **Export Spreadsheet (.CSV):** Generates a complete box score and raw timestamped play-by-play log. Opens directly in **Apple Numbers**, **Google Sheets**, or **Microsoft Excel**.
* **Print / PDF Report:** One-click generation of a clean, professional 1-page printable game report complete with period-by-period shot totals, skater stats, goalie save percentage, and coach notes area. Ready to **AirDrop**, text via **iMessage**, or email straight to the coach right after the final buzzer.
